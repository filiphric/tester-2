# <Highlighter>What is left for us?</Highlighter>

<!-- 
- I talked to a lot of developers and testers over the past few years that are facing difficult times
- the skill they have been perfecting over the years is suddenly being taken away from them
- the better the models get and the better capabilities they have, the more we question what is left of us
- so what is left of us?

- whenever we see a great sportsman, amazing art or great piece of engineering, it makes us feel something
- have you ever stopped to think about why that is? 
- why do we amazement when we see two amazing teams face each other in the world cup?
- why we feel nothing when we see a picture generated by AI but marvel at the ceiling of a sistine chapel?
- why is it that a game between two chess grandmasters is more entertaining than watching two computers play?
- why does reading Lord of the Rings touch our hearts, but AI generated text feels empty?

- but I actually think the reason for that is hope
