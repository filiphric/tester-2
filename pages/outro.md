# <Highlighter>What is left for us?</Highlighter>

<!-- 
- I talked to a lot of developers and testers over the past few years that are facing difficult times
- the skill they have been perfecting over the years is suddenly being taken away from them
- the better the models get and the better capabilities they have, the more we question what is left for us
- What do we do with the fact that we now live with super intelligent machines?
- I am not sure if I have a clear or satisfying answer to that
- I only have hints of an answer
- whenever we see a great sportsman, amazing art or great piece of engineering, it makes us feel something
- have you ever stopped to think about why that is? 
- why do we feel amazement when we see two great teams face each other in the world cup?
- why we feel nothing when we see a picture generated by AI but marvel at the ceiling of the sistine chapel?
- why is it that a game between two chess grandmasters is more entertaining than watching two computers play?
- why does reading Lord of the Rings touch our hearts, but AI generated text feels empty?
- I think - on a conference like this some of you may say the reason is quality
- but I actually think the reason for that is hope
- when we see other people achieve great things, there’s a little part of us that makes us believe we are capable of great things too

# <Highlighter>Becoming indispensable.</Highlighter>
